How to Use the Demo Guide

Find

Use Search, Module, or Value Driver filters to quickly narrow the vignette library.

Preview

Click Preview to see the staged screenshot before entering the demo environment.

Build your flow

Add individual vignettes, add your filtered results, or start with a Recommended Flow.

Reorder

Open My Demo Flow and drag vignettes into the exact order you want to present them.

Walk through

Launch Guided Walkthrough to move through your demo one vignette at a time.

Make it yours

Use the prominent Show / hide controls in Grid, Matrix, My Demo Flow, or Guided Walkthrough to toggle Where it lives, Key Value, and Notes on or off. Add personal notes for transitions, discovery reminders, or your own talk track.

Your GPS for the Demo Pioneers environment

Get up to speed quickly on what’s been built and how to use it. Explore screenshots, click paths, and value propositions to find the right stories for your next demo, and confidently guide prospects through the same experience when the environment is cloned for a trial.
